📚 Learn Crypto

What is Bitcoin?

Bitcoin is a digital asset that uses a blockchain network to record transactions. Its market price can move up and down quickly.

What is an entry line?

In this demo, your entry line is the selected price at the moment you press UP or DOWN. It is a visual learning tool, not a real trading order.

UP / DOWN result

  • UP: above entry = WIN.
  • UP: below entry = LOSE.
  • DOWN: below entry = WIN.
  • DOWN: above entry = LOSE.
  • Same price = WAIT.

Risk basics

Crypto prices are volatile. Never assume a price must go up or down. This app uses simulated prices and demo balance only.
